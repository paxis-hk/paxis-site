Request a quote · received

Thank you. We have your request.

The next steps are set out below.

  1. We reply by email from or@paxis-hk.com with instructions for sending your drawings. If you require an NDA first, please state this in your reply.
  2. Send your drawing package by email: PDF drawing with tolerances and revision, STEP file if you have one, material, finish, quantities and target date.
  3. We review it, raise any questions, and select the right qualified factory. You then receive one quotation from PAXIS, in US dollars.

If our reply has not arrived, please check your spam folder, or write to or@paxis-hk.com.
