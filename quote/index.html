    What happens next

    1. We reply by email from or@paxis-hk.com and confirm the information needed for the quotation.

    2. Drawings and specifications, if you have them, can be emailed at any time, with quantities, material, finish and target date. If you require an NDA, we sign it before you send drawings.

    3. We review the drawing and raise any questions, then select the right qualified factory for your part.

    4. You receive one quotation from PAXIS, in US dollars. Quotations are issued within two to three working days of receiving complete drawings and specifications; complex projects may require additional engineering review.

    Useful for the quotation

    • PDF drawing with tolerances, revision and critical dimensions marked
    • 3D model (STEP) if you have one
    • Material and finish
    • Quantity per order, and per year if it repeats
    • Documents you need: inspection report, material certificate, others
    • Target date
    • Any confidentiality or export requirements

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you produce single prototypes?

    Yes. A project can begin with a few prototype pieces or a first-article lot, for example 10 pieces, before series production. Where a process has a practical minimum because of setup or tooling, this is stated in the quotation.

    Is there a minimum order?

    No fixed minimum. We quote from a few prototype pieces to tens of thousands of parts in scheduled batches. Where a very small quantity is uneconomical for a process, this is stated before the quotation.

    How do you choose the factory?

    We select the right qualified factory for your part: the process, equipment and experience it needs, with the best quality at the most competitive price. You receive one quotation from PAXIS.

    How do I pay?

    You pay PAXIS in US dollars. There are no currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China. Payment terms are stated in each quotation.

    How do I send drawings?

    Drawings are optional for an enquiry, which can be made by the form or by email. If you have drawings or specifications, you are welcome to email them to or@paxis-hk.com whenever convenient, quoting your company name. They are used only for your enquiry and order, and go only to the factory selected for your part. Password-protected files are welcome; send the password separately.

    Do you sign NDAs?

    Yes, on request, before you send drawings. Drawings are used only for your enquiry and order, and go only to the factory selected for your part.

    Who is responsible for export classification?

    You are responsible for the export classification of your drawings. We cannot accept drawings that may not legally be manufactured in China.

    How do you check the parts?

    The factory inspects to your drawing or QC sheet and supplies a report. Our team then carries out an independent quality verification at the factory, after production and before the goods are released, and the parts are photographed before and after packing. Where you specify 100% inspection, every part is inspected.

    Which documents are supplied?

    An inspection report to your drawing or QC sheet, a first-article report when first articles are made, material reports or certificates where required, and photos before and after packing. Material compliance documents on request.

    How long does production take?

    The lead time depends on the part and is stated in the quotation, together with the date the order will be ready at the factory. Factories in China close for several weeks around Chinese New Year; orders are scheduled accordingly.

    What does EXW mean for my order?

    Your order is ready at the factory. Your own forwarder or courier collects it, and we assist with coordinating the collection. Transport after collection is arranged by you. See how an order runs.

    Custom parts manufactured to your drawing in vetted partner factories in southern and eastern China, supervised and inspected by our own team.
