    Our team in China

    What happens next

    1. We reply by email and tell you where to send your drawings.

    2. You email your drawings with quantities, material, finish and target date. If you need an NDA first, we sign yours before you send anything.

    3. We review the drawing and ask any questions, then select the right qualified factory for your part.

    4. You get one quote from PAXIS, in US dollars. How long that takes depends on the part; we tell you when to expect it.

    What to send with your drawings

    • PDF drawing with tolerances, revision and critical dimensions marked
    • 3D model (STEP) if you have one
    • Material and finish
    • Quantity per order, and per year if it repeats
    • Documents you need: inspection report, material certificate, others
    • Target date
    • Any confidentiality or export requirements

    Questions buyers ask us

    Do you produce single prototypes?

    Yes. Many jobs start with a few prototype or first-article pieces, often about 10, before series production. Some processes have a practical minimum because of setup or tooling; we tell you at the quote stage.

    Is there a minimum order quantity?

    Very small runs can be uneconomic for some factories, and tooled parts (molds, dies) only make sense above a certain volume. We will say so honestly rather than quote a poor price.

    How do you choose the factory?

    We select the right qualified factory for your part: the process, equipment and experience it needs, with the best quality at the most competitive price. You receive one quote from PAXIS.

    How do I pay?

    You pay PAXIS in US dollars. There are no currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China.

    How do I send drawings, and who sees them?

    By email only, after we reply to your request. Drawings go only to the factory we select for your part. If you need an NDA, we sign yours on request before you send anything. Password-protected files are welcome. You are responsible for the export classification of your drawings; we cannot accept drawings that may not legally be manufactured in China.

    How do you check the parts?

    The factory inspects to your drawing or QC sheet and supplies a report. Our team then verifies quality at the factory, with 100% inspection of appearance and dimensions on early orders. New parts go through first articles when needed. Parts are photographed before and after packing. See Quality.

    Which documents do I get?

    Inspection reports against your drawing, first-article reports for new parts, and material reports or certificates where required. Material compliance documents on request. Tell us at RFQ what you need, so it is part of the quote.

    What happens if parts are out of spec?

    Tell us with photos or measurements. We work out root cause and corrective action with the factory and confirm the fix with you.

    What are typical lead times?

    Production typically takes about 30 days from PO. Tooling projects typically take about 90 days to first samples. Both depend on the part, finish and quantity, and exclude transport after collection. Factories close for about two to four weeks around Chinese New Year.

    Who arranges transport?

    We work EXW: your order is ready for collection at the factory in China. Your own forwarder or courier collects it, and we help them arrange the collection. We do not ship the goods ourselves.
