Legal · Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last reviewed: 5 November 2026

HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD (PAXIS) intends paxis-hk.com to be usable by everyone, including people who use a screen reader, the keyboard alone, screen magnification, voice control or other assistive technology.

Standard

The website targets conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). Conformance with WCAG 2.2 level AA also meets level AA of WCAG 2.1 and 2.0, on which the European standard EN 301 549 and the United States Section 508 requirements are based.

Conformance status

We are not aware of any failure of WCAG 2.2 level AA on this website. Full conformance will be confirmed once the tests listed under "Known limitations" are completed.

Accessibility features

  • Every page can be used with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link. The fixed header does not cover the focused element.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks, lists and table headers in a logical order. Images that carry information have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and interface elements meet the WCAG contrast ratios. Buttons and links are at least 24 by 24 pixels or adequately spaced.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200%, and the pages reflow without sideways scrolling down to a width of 320 pixels (400% zoom).
  • The short video clips have no sound, can be paused, and do not play when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Every form field has a visible label. Errors are described in text and listed at the top of the form with a link to each field.
  • No third-party accessibility overlay is used.

How the website was evaluated

On 5 November 2026 every page was evaluated against each WCAG 2.2 level A and AA success criterion:

  • automated tests in the Chromium browser engine (used by Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge) at screen widths of 1440, 390 and 320 pixels, covering page structure, text alternatives, names of links and buttons, form labels and error messages, color contrast, target size, focus visibility, keyboard order, text spacing, enlarged text and page zoom;
  • keyboard tests of the menus, the frequently asked questions, the video clips and the quote form;
  • a review of the accessibility tree, the information that screen readers present, for representative pages.

These tests run again before every update of the website.

Known limitations

  • The website has not yet been tested with screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) or in the Firefox and Safari browsers. These tests are planned, and this statement will be updated with the results.
  • Documents we send by email, such as quotations, inspection reports and material certificates, are produced by our partner factories or by third-party software and may not be fully accessible. On request, we provide the information in another format.

Feedback and contact

If you encounter a barrier on this website, or need information in another format, write to or@paxis-hk.com with "Accessibility" in the subject line, and state the page address and the problem. We aim to reply within 5 working days and to remove the barrier or provide the information in another way.
