How an order runs

You deal with PAXIS from the first question until your order is ready for collection. Behind that sits a network of vetted factories in China and our own team in China, who supervise production and verify quality at those factories week by week.

Nine steps from RFQ to collection

  1. RFQ and drawings

    You fill in the short form or email us directly. We reply with where to send your drawings. Send a PDF drawing with tolerances and revision, a 3D model (STEP) if you have one, material, finish, quantities and your target date.

    You get: a confirmation. If you need an NDA first, we sign yours before you send anything.

  2. Drawing review

    Before we quote, our team and the factory read the drawing and raise questions: material availability, hardness, thread depth, coating contact points, clamping points. We translate them and send them to you. Any change is approved by you in writing.

    You get: a short list of questions, or a clear "no questions".

  3. Factory selection

    We select the right qualified factory for your part: the process, equipment and experience it needs, with the best quality at the most competitive price.

    You get: nothing yet. This step is our work.

  4. One quote from PAXIS

    One price per part and quantity, in US dollars, with typical lead time and EXW terms.

    You get: a written quote from PAXIS.

  5. Purchase order and payment

    You place one PO with PAXIS and pay PAXIS in US dollars: no currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China. We place the order with the selected factory and agree the schedule with you.

    You get: an order confirmation with dates.

  6. First articles

    For new parts, the factory produces first-article samples (often about 10 pieces) before series production, when needed. Production starts after you approve them.

    You get: samples and an inspection report against your drawing.

  7. Production and quality control

    The factory manufactures your parts and inspects them to your drawing or QC sheet. Our team supervises production at the factory, follows the agreed schedule and verifies quality. On early orders we require 100% inspection of appearance and dimensions, not sampling.

    You get: inspection reports, and material reports or certificates where required.

  8. Packing and collection (EXW)

    Our team checks the parts and photographs them before and after packing. Your order is then ready at the factory, EXW. Your own forwarder or courier collects it, and we help them arrange the collection.

    You get: packing photos, and help to arrange collection.

  9. Repeat orders and problems

    Repeat orders run with the same factory and the same inspection requirements, unless you approve a change. If a part is out of spec, we work out the root cause and corrective action with the factory and confirm the fix with you.

    You get: one contact for the whole history of the part.

Our factory network

We don't own factories, and we don't pretend to. We work with vetted factories in southern and eastern China, most of them in Guangdong. Each job goes to the qualified factory that suits the process, material and quantity, and finishing work such as anodizing or coating goes to specialist shops the factory works with.

Factories stay in the network on results: drawing questions raised early, first articles that pass, reports that match the parts.

Who you talk to

  • One contact at PAXIS for quotes, orders and problems, by email at or@paxis-hk.com.
  • Our team in China speaks with the factories in Chinese and with you in English.
  • One quote and one invoice from PAXIS, paid in US dollars.
  • Status updates by email, with photos from the factory when something needs your decision.
  • Questions from the factory are passed to you in writing, with a clear question and options, not a forwarded chat.

When we are a good fit

Good fit

  • Custom parts manufactured to your drawing (build-to-print)
  • Prototypes that will go on to repeat production
  • From a few prototype pieces to tens of thousands of parts
  • Parts that need a finish, a material report or an inspection report
  • Kits that combine parts from several processes

Not a good fit

  • Parts with no drawing, where you need us to design them
  • Next-day emergency parts
