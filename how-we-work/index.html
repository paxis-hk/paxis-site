How we work · from RFQ to EXW

How an order runs

You deal with PAXIS from the first question until your order is ready for collection. Behind that sits a network of vetted partner factories in southern and eastern China and our own team in China, who supervise production and verify quality at those factories.

Nine steps

What happens, who acts, and what you receive

  1. 01

    You send an RFQ

    By the form or by email. Drawings are optional at this stage and welcome whenever convenient: PDF with tolerances and revision, STEP if you have it, plus quantities, material and finish. If you require an NDA, we sign it before you send any drawings. The RFQ checklist lists what to send.

    Who actsYou

    You receiveConfirmation of receipt, and a list of any missing information.

  2. 02

    We review the drawing

    Questions are raised before the quotation: material availability, hardness, coating contact points, thread depth, clamping points. Any change is approved by you in writing.

    Who actsPAXIS + factory

    You receiveWritten questions or proposals, or written confirmation that there are none.

  3. 03

    We select the factory and quote

    We place the part with the qualified factory equipped for its process: the best quality at the most competitive price.

    Who actsPAXIS

    You receiveOne quotation from PAXIS, in US dollars, with the lead time per quantity and EXW terms.

  4. 04

    You place the PO

    Your PO is placed with PAXIS, and you pay PAXIS in US dollars. We place the order with the factory, together with your drawing and QC sheet.

    Who actsYou + PAXIS

    You receiveOrder confirmation with the agreed schedule.

  5. 05

    First articles

    Where required, the factory produces first articles, for example 10 pieces, before series production.

    Who actsFactory

    You receiveFirst-article parts and their report. Series production is released after your written confirmation.

  6. 06

    Production, supervised

    Our team in China follows production at the factory against the agreed schedule.

    Who actsFactory + PAXIS

    You receiveStatus updates against the agreed schedule.

  7. 07

    Inspection

    The factory performs its final inspection to your drawing or QC sheet. Our team then carries out an independent quality verification at the factory before the goods are released. See the two independent stages of inspection.

    Who actsFactory + PAXIS

    You receiveInspection report; material reports or certificates where required.

  8. 08

    Packing checked

    Our team checks the parts before they are packed and records the packing.

    Who actsPAXIS

    You receivePhotos before and after packing.

  9. 09

    Ready at the factory, EXW

    The order is ready at the factory. Your forwarder or courier collects it, and we assist with coordinating the collection.

    Who actsYou + PAXIS

    You receiveCollection details for your forwarder, and one invoice in US dollars.

Payment

One invoice, in US dollars

You receive one quotation and one invoice from PAXIS and pay PAXIS in US dollars. No currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China. Payment terms are stated in each quotation.

EXW

Ready at the factory

We sell EXW: your order is ready at the factory in China. Your own forwarder or courier collects it, and our team assists with coordinating the collection. Transport after collection is arranged by you.

Planning

Chinese New Year

Factories in China close for several weeks around Chinese New Year, in January or February. Orders placed shortly before the holiday are completed after it. The cut-off dates for your parts are confirmed when you order.

FAQ

Questions about ordering

Is there a minimum order?

No fixed minimum. We quote from a few prototype pieces to tens of thousands of parts in scheduled batches. Where a very small quantity is uneconomical for a process, this is stated before the quotation.

What do you need for a quote?

The drawing as PDF with tolerances and revision, a STEP file if you have one, material, finish and the quantities you want priced. Email them to or@paxis-hk.com whenever convenient; an enquiry can also start with the short form, without drawings. The RFQ checklist sets out the complete list.

How quickly do you quote?

Quotations are issued within two to three working days of receiving complete drawings and specifications; complex projects may require additional engineering review. Any drawing questions are raised before the quotation.

Do you produce single prototypes?

Yes. A project can begin with a few prototype pieces or a first-article lot, for example 10 pieces, before series production. Where a process has a practical minimum because of setup or tooling, this is stated in the quotation.

How long does production take?

The lead time depends on the part and is stated in the quotation, together with the date the order will be ready at the factory. Factories in China close for several weeks around Chinese New Year; orders are scheduled accordingly.

How do repeat orders work?

Repeat orders run with the same factory, your drawing revision and the same inspection requirements, unless you approve a change.

Is PAXIS a factory or a sourcing agent?

PAXIS is a Hong Kong company that takes your order and is responsible for the parts. The parts are manufactured in vetted partner factories in China, supervised and verified by our own team in China. You receive one quotation and pay PAXIS in US dollars.

What does EXW mean for my order?

Your order is ready at the factory. Your own forwarder or courier collects it, and we assist with coordinating the collection. Transport after collection is arranged by you. See how an order runs.

Do you arrange freight?

No. Orders are sold EXW. We help your forwarder arrange collection at the factory.

How do I pay?

You pay PAXIS in US dollars. There are no currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China. Payment terms are stated in each quotation.
