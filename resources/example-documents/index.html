Resources · Example documents

Example documents

A drawing goes in, reports come back. Both are shown here as examples so you know the format before your first order. All values and part numbers are fictitious; none is a real part or customer.

Request a quoteQuality control

You send

The drawing

A PDF drawing with tolerances, datums and the revision letter. A STEP model is welcome; the PDF remains the master unless you specify otherwise. The example shows the title-block fields we look for: drawing number, revision, material, finish and the general tolerance note.

  • Tolerances on every functional dimension
  • Datums and geometric tolerances where they matter
  • Material grade and temper; finish and color
  • Revision letter, in the title block and the file name

You receive

First-article report

When first articles are produced (the quantity is agreed for each part, for example 10 pieces), each characteristic on your drawing is measured on each piece and reported against nominal and tolerance. Series production starts after you confirm the first articles.

Header
Part, drawing number and revision, material, finish, lot size
Characteristics
Numbered to match the balloons on your drawing, or your QC sheet
Result
Measured value and OK / not OK per characteristic

You receive

Inspection report

Every production order comes with an inspection report from the factory, to your drawing or QC sheet. Our team then verifies the parts and the report at the factory, independently of the factory, before the goods are released.

You receive

Packing photo record

Before the order is released for collection, our team photographs the parts before and after packing. The photos are sent to you with the documents.

What the photo record shows
PhotoShows
Before packingThe parts laid out, with the quantity visible, and close-ups of cosmetic faces.
Inner packingHow the parts are separated and protected: bags, foam, dividers.
After packingClosed cartons with their markings, ready at the factory for your forwarder.

On request

Material reports and certificates

Where your drawing or PO requires it, a material report or certificate is supplied with the parts. Please state this with the RFQ, so that it is included in the quotation and the factory order. Material compliance documents on request.
