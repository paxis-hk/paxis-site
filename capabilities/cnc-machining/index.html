CNC machining to your drawing

Most of the RFQs we receive are for CNC machined parts, so this is the process where our partner factories and our team have the most experience. Parts are milled, turned and, where the geometry calls for it, machined on 5-axis centers in one setup. They are manufactured to your drawing, inspected by the factory and verified by our team in China.

02

Milling, 5-axis and turning

  • CNC milling

    Prismatic parts such as housings, plates, brackets and holders, with pockets, bores, threads and datum faces machined to your drawing. Deep pockets and thin walls are reviewed at the drawing stage: if an inside surface finish is hard to reach with the tool length the geometry allows, this is raised before the quotation.

  • 5-axis machining

    Compound angles and features on several faces, machined in one setup instead of several. Fewer setups mean fewer re-clamping errors between features that relate to each other. 5-axis centers are also used to engrave lettering on anodized parts where laser marking is not wanted.

  • CNC turning

    Pins, shafts, rings, spacers and round holders, including knurling and small hobbed teeth. For toothed parts the factory checks the tooth count and pitch against your drawing and confirms them with you before production.

  • Secondary operations

    Chamfering and grinding, tapped and press-fit holes, and re-machining after coating so that contact faces stay bare where masking is not reliable.

05

Tolerances and inspection

Tolerances come from your drawing and specification. Your drawings go to the production engineers of the factory selected for your part. If a tolerance or a feature cannot be held, we raise it before production, and any change to the drawing is confirmed and approved by you in writing. Production is to the drawing.

Critical dimensions that you circle, or that your QC sheet lists, are inspected and reported. Where a tolerance appears tighter than the function requires, it is raised at drawing review, before the quotation, for your decision.

Inspection and documents

The factory inspects each order to your drawing or QC sheet and supplies a report. Our team in China then carries out an independent quality verification at the factory, after production and before the goods are released, and the packing is photographed. Where you specify 100% inspection, every part is inspected. Where first articles are required, series production is released only after your written confirmation. Material reports or certificates are supplied where your drawing or PO requires them.

06

Design notes

  1. 1

    From a past order

    Specify hardness and grade together.

    Full-hard 316 is not always available in China. On one order the factory proposed half-hard 316, and the customer approved it in writing before production started.

  2. 2

    Engineering note

    Mark the faces that must stay bare.

    If a coated part has electrical or locating contact faces, show them on the drawing. The factory can mask them, or re-machine them after coating.

  3. 3

    From a past order

    Give deep pockets a realistic roughness.

    A long tool in a deep pocket leaves a rougher surface than the outside faces. If the inside surface is functional, specify it; its feasibility is confirmed before the quotation.

  4. 4

    Engineering note

    Name the black finish.

    Black oxide, black anodizing, black PVD and QPQ are different processes with different appearance, thickness and corrosion behavior. The process named on the drawing is the one used; any change requires your written approval.

  5. 5

    Engineering note

    Send the PDF as the master.

    STEP files are welcome for programming. Where they disagree, the PDF drawing and its revision letter govern unless you specify otherwise.

09

Questions

Do you manufacture 5-axis machined parts?

Yes. Parts with features on several faces or compound angles are placed with factories that machine them on 5-axis centers, in one setup where the geometry allows.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

There is no fixed minimum. The first-article quantity is agreed for each part, for example 10 pieces. Where a very small quantity is uneconomical for a process, this is stated before the quotation.

Can a material grade be substituted?

Only with your written approval. If a grade is not readily available, this is stated before the quotation, with an alternative.

Which tolerance standard do you work to?

The one on your drawing. The factory inspects to your drawing and the standards it calls up, and our team verifies the parts against the same drawing.

What documents come with the parts?

An inspection report to your drawing or QC sheet, a first-article report when first articles are made, material reports or certificates where required, and photos before and after packing.
