04Materials

Materials

We work in the grade and temper your drawing calls for. We do not substitute a material without your written approval. When a grade is not readily available in China, this is stated before the quotation, together with an alternative for your written approval.

  • Metals: aluminum, copper, brass, bronze, iron and steel (including alloy, tool and mold steels), stainless steel.
  • Plastics: technical plastics, machined, cast or injection molded.
  • Specialized: composites and technical textiles.

05Finishes

Finishes and heat treatment

Finishes are applied by specialist shops that work with the factory, and they are part of your quote and your inspection. Masked faces, coating contact points and faces re-machined after coating are agreed with you before production.

  • Anodizing, including black
  • Black oxide
  • Passivation
  • PVD coating
  • Heat treatment and hardening
  • Painting (PU and epoxy)
  • Silk-screen printing
  • Others on request

08RFQ

What to send for a quote

  • Drawing as PDFWith all tolerances, the revision letter and the general tolerance standard you work to.
  • 3D modelSTEP if you have one. The PDF remains the master unless you specify otherwise.
  • MaterialGrade and temper or hardness. State whether an equivalent grade is acceptable.
  • FinishProcess, color and thickness; mark faces that must stay bare or be masked.
  • QuantitiesFirst articles, first batch and expected yearly volume, so that each quantity can be priced.
  • Critical dimensionsCircle them, or send your QC sheet. These are inspected and reported.
  • Documents you needInspection report, first-article report, material certificate.
  • Your NDAIf an NDA is required before drawings are sent, please send it first.

09FAQ

Questions about capabilities

Can you manufacture parts that need a process or material not listed?

Yes. The processes on this site are our main capabilities, not a closed list. Through our network of vetted partner factories we manufacture across the full range of technologies and materials available in Chinese industry. If it can be manufactured in China, we can manufacture it to your drawing. Send the drawing; the manufacturing route is confirmed in writing with the quotation.

Can a material grade be substituted?

Only with your written approval. If a grade is not readily available in China, this is stated before the quotation, together with a proposed alternative.

Who arranges finishing?

Finishes are applied by specialist shops that work with the factory. Coating contact points and masked areas are agreed with you before production.

What if my drawing calls up a specific standard?

We place the order with a factory that works to it, and the parts are inspected to it. If a requirement cannot be met, this is stated before the quotation, together with a proposed alternative.

Which documents are supplied?

An inspection report to your drawing or QC sheet, a first-article report when first articles are made, material reports or certificates where required, and photos before and after packing. Material compliance documents on request.
