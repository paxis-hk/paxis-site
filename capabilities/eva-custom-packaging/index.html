  • Foam inserts

Tolerances and inspection

Tolerances come from your drawing and specification. Anything that cannot be held is raised before production, and any drawing change is approved by you in writing.

Cut-outs are made to your layout or DXF and checked against your parts when the sample is approved. The approved sample is the reference for the run.

Inspection and documents

Two stages: the factory inspects to your drawing or QC sheet, then our own team verifies the order at the factory before release. 100% inspection is carried out where you specify it.

Design notes

    Name the master file.

    Send the DXF and the PDF together, and state which is the master. Unless you specify otherwise, the PDF is the master.

  2. Engineering note

    Approve a sample first.

    Foam and print differ slightly between suppliers. A sample with your parts in it is approved before the run.

Questions

Can you pack our parts into the packaging?

Yes. Parts from several processes can be packed into one insert as a kit, so the kit is ready at the factory.

Do we approve a sample?

Yes. A sample is approved before the run, and it becomes the reference.

Is there a minimum quantity for custom EVA inserts?

Inserts are produced to your order quantity. Where a very small quantity is uneconomical, this is stated before the quotation.
