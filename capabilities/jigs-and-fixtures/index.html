Fixture types

  • Machining fixtures

    Base plates, nests and clamps that hold your part repeatably on a machine table.

  • Welding fixtures

    Frames and locators that hold sheet-metal parts in position during welding and control distortion.

  • Riveting and assembly fixtures

    Nests and stops that keep rivets flush and parts aligned, batch after batch.

  • Inspection fixtures

    Go/no-go and checking fixtures that allow your incoming inspection to check a part quickly.

Tolerances and inspection

Tolerances come from your drawing and specification. Anything that cannot be held is raised before production, and any drawing change is approved by you in writing.

Fixtures are manufactured to your drawing. Locating pins, nests and datum faces are inspected and reported, because they decide the accuracy of every part the fixture holds.

If you send a concept rather than a drawing, the fixture drawing is agreed with you in writing before manufacture.

Inspection and documents

Two stages: the factory inspects to your drawing or QC sheet, then our own team verifies the order at the factory before release. 100% inspection is carried out where you specify it.

Design notes

Show the design notes for jigs and fixtures

  1. Engineering note

    Fixtures for assembly steps.

    Riveting, pressing and bonding steps are repeatable when the part is held in a fixture built for it. State which assembly steps need one, and it is included in the quotation.

  2. Engineering note

    Harden all wear parts.

    Locating pins and wear plates touch every part. Specify their hardness and make them replaceable.

  3. Engineering note

    Mark the datums.

    Show which faces and pins define the part position, so they are inspected and reported.

Questions

Can you design a fixture from my part drawing?

A concept is agreed with you and drawn by the factory. You approve the fixture drawing in writing before it is manufactured.

Do you build fixtures for parts you also produce?

Yes. Welding and riveting fixtures for our sheet-metal assemblies are built by the same factories.
