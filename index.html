Capabilities

What we manufacture

Through our network of vetted partner factories we manufacture across the full range of technologies and materials available in Chinese industry, from a few prototype pieces to tens of thousands of parts in scheduled batches. Our main capabilities are shown below; for any other process or material, ask us. Our customers are equipment makers and OEMs: see the industries we manufacture for.

CNC machining

Milled · turned · 5-axis

Housings, holders, plates, pins, shafts and rings in aluminum, stainless steel, steel, brass, copper and technical plastics.

QuantitiesFrom about 10 first articles to production lots of thousands

View process

Sheet metal and laser cutting

Laser-cut · bent · welded · riveted

Brackets, panels, covers and enclosures, laser-cut, bent on a press brake, welded or riveted, with fixtures built for your part.

QuantitiesPrototype sets to production batches

View process

Plastic parts and injection molds

Machined · cast · molded

Covers, enclosures and technical parts: machined or cast for low volumes, injection molded in a mold built for your part for production.

QuantitiesPrototypes, then hundreds to thousands per lot

View process
Illustration

Custom springs

Coiled · ground · finished

Compression and extension springs produced to your drawing by a specialist spring manufacturer in our network.

QuantitiesPrototype quantities to production lots

View process

Jigs and fixtures

Machined · hardened · assembled

Base plates, locating pins, clamps and nests, and the welding and riveting fixtures that keep repeat batches identical.

QuantitiesSingle fixtures or small sets

EVA and custom packaging

Cut · printed · packed

Foam inserts that hold each part in place, cases, printed boxes with your artwork, and protective films cut to your DXF.

QuantitiesTo your order quantity

Assemblies and kits

Riveted · fitted · kitted

Riveted and welded sheet-metal assemblies, and kits of matching metal and plastic parts, painted to match and packed as one unit.

QuantitiesKits and sub-assemblies in scheduled batches

Quality

Two independent stages of inspection

  1. Stage 1 · ManufacturerIn-process and final inspection to your drawingPerformed by the factory and reported against your drawing or QC sheet.
  2. Stage 2 · PAXISIndependent quality verificationBy our own team at the factory, after production and before the goods are released; packing checked and photographed.
  • Inspection reportTo your drawing or QC sheet, issued by the factory and verified by our team.
  • First-article reportWhen first articles are produced, before series production starts.
