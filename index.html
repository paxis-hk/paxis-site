02Who we are

A Hong Kong company manufacturing to your drawing in China

PAXIS is the trade name of HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD, a Hong Kong company under Western ownership. We manufacture custom parts to customer drawings: machined, fabricated, molded and assembled in a network of partner factories in southern and eastern China that we have qualified for their processes.

You send your RFQ and your PO to PAXIS, and you pay PAXIS in US dollars. We place the work with the right factory for the part. Our own team in China follows production to the agreed schedule and verifies quality at the factory before the parts are packed. You have one supplier and one contact, and that contact is responsible for the result.

08FAQ

Before you send a drawing

Is there a minimum order?

No fixed minimum. We quote from a few prototype pieces to tens of thousands of parts in scheduled batches. Where a very small quantity is uneconomical for a process, this is stated before the quotation.

What do you need for a quote?

The drawing as PDF with tolerances and revision, a STEP file if you have one, material, finish and the quantities you want priced. Send them to or@paxis-hk.com. The RFQ checklist sets out the complete list.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, on request, before you send drawings. Drawings travel by email only and go only to the factory selected for your part.

What does EXW mean for my order?

Your order is ready at the factory. Your own forwarder or courier collects it, and we assist with coordinating the collection. Transport after collection is arranged by you.

All questions

Send us your drawing

Email the PDF and STEP files with quantities, material and finish to or@paxis-hk.com, or start with the short form. We confirm receipt and raise any drawing questions before the quotation.
