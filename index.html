One supplier for parts manufactured to your drawing

PAXIS is a Hong Kong company under Western ownership. We manufacture custom parts to customer drawings through a network of vetted factories in southern and eastern China.

Our own team in China supervises production at those factories. It follows the agreed schedule, verifies quality against your drawing and checks the parts before they are packed. You send one RFQ and one PO to PAXIS, and you deal with one contact who answers for the result.

What you get with PAXIS

Access to the quality, precision and prices of China's manufacturing, without managing factories in China yourself.

Quality
Every order is inspected twice: by the factory, against your drawing or QC sheet, and by our team at the factory before packing.
Precision
Each part goes to a factory equipped for its process and tolerances, from CNC and 5-axis machining to laser cutting and injection molding.
On schedule
We commit to the schedule agreed in your order. Our team follows production at the factory and tells you early if a date is at risk.
Price
We select the right qualified factory for your part: the best quality at the most competitive price. You receive one quote from PAXIS.
Payment in US dollars
You pay PAXIS in US dollars. No currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China.

How an order runs

The full process, step by step

  1. You send an RFQ.Drawings by email, with quantities, material and finish.
  2. We review the drawing.Questions are raised and answered before we quote. Any change is approved by you in writing.
  3. We select the right factory.The qualified factory with the best quality at the most competitive price for your part. You receive one quote from PAXIS.
  4. Our team supervises production.First articles when needed, then the series, to the agreed schedule.
  5. Inspected, packed, ready at the factory.Inspection reports and photos before and after packing. Ready at the factory, EXW – we help your forwarder arrange collection.

Manufacturing in China, with the risk managed

Most buyers have the same five questions before they send a drawing to China. Here is what we do about each.

Will the parts match the drawing?
The factory inspects to your drawing or QC sheet and supplies a report. Our team then verifies quality at the factory. On early orders that means 100% inspection, not sampling.
Will my drawings stay private?
Drawings travel by email only, never through an upload portal. They go only to the factory we select for your part. We sign your NDA on request.
Will anything change without my knowing?
No change to material, finish or process goes ahead without your written approval.
Who do I talk to when something goes wrong?
You talk to PAXIS. We work out the root cause and corrective action with the factory, and confirm the fix with you.
Am I paying a fair factory price?
We select the right qualified factory for your part: the best quality at the most competitive price. You receive one quote from PAXIS and pay in US dollars.
