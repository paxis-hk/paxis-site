Quality · two independent stages

Quality control at the factory, to your drawing

Quality control at PAXIS has two independent stages. You place your order with PAXIS, and PAXIS is responsible for the parts. The factory inspects every order to your drawing and writes the report. Our team in China then carries out an independent quality verification at the factory. The goods are released only after this verification.

Two stages

The manufacturer inspects. PAXIS verifies.

Quality on a PAXIS order is controlled in two independent stages. The manufacturer inspects its own work to your drawing. Our own team then verifies the parts and the manufacturer's report at the factory, independently of the manufacturer.

  1. Stage 1 · ManufacturerIn-process and final inspection to your drawingThe factory inspects the parts during production and at final inspection, with its own instruments, and reports the results against your drawing or QC sheet.
  2. Stage 2 · PAXISIndependent quality verification at the factoryAfter production, our own team verifies the parts and the manufacturer's report at the factory. The goods are released only after this verification.

Release

Released only after verification

Stage 2 is independent of the manufacturer. Our own team verifies the parts and the manufacturer's inspection report at the factory, after production. The goods are released for collection only after this verification is complete.

Where your drawing, QC sheet or purchase order specifies 100% inspection, every part is inspected and the results are reported. Please state this requirement with the RFQ, so that it is included in the quotation and the schedule.

Checkpoints

Four control points

  1. 1

    Before the quotation

    The drawing is reviewed and questions are raised: material, hardness, threads, coating contact points, inside surface finish. Any change is approved by you in writing before production.

  2. 2

    First articles

    For new parts, first articles (for example 10 pieces) are produced and inspected before series production, where required. Series production is released only after your written confirmation.

  3. 3

    During and after production

    The factory inspects to your drawing or QC sheet. Our team supervises production at the factory and follows the agreed schedule.

  4. 4

    Before packing

    Our team verifies the parts at the factory after production. The packing is checked and photographed, and the goods are released only after this verification.

Measurement

Who measures what

Measurements are made at the factory, with the factory's instruments, and reported against your drawing. Our team verifies the parts and the reports at the factory. PAXIS does not operate its own measuring laboratory and does not claim certificates held by others.

Factories

How factories are selected and kept

Factories are qualified after a review of their capabilities, their portfolio of past work, their production quality and sample parts, a visit to the factory and their reputation. We visit the factories several times a year. For your part we select the right qualified factory: the best quality at the most competitive price. Our network of vetted partner factories

Changes

No silent changes

No change to material, finish, process or drawing interpretation goes ahead without your written approval. If a material is not available, or a finish requires a contact point on a visible face, your approval is obtained first.

Confidentiality

Confidentiality of your drawings

Drawings are used only for your enquiry and order, and go only to the factory we select for your part. We sign your NDA on request, before you send drawings.

Standards

What Western standards means here

We work to your drawing and the standards it calls up. When your drawing or PO requires a specific standard or material certificates, we place the order with a factory that works to it. If a requirement cannot be met, this is stated before the quotation, together with a proposed alternative.

FAQ

Questions about quality

How do you check the parts?

The factory inspects to your drawing or QC sheet and supplies a report. Our team then carries out an independent quality verification at the factory, after production and before the goods are released, and the parts are photographed before and after packing. Where you specify 100% inspection, every part is inspected.

Can every part be inspected?

Yes. Where you specify 100% inspection, every part is inspected and the results are reported. Please state this requirement with the RFQ, so that it is included in the quotation and the schedule.

Which tolerances can you hold?

The tolerances on your drawing and specification. Your drawings go to the production engineers of the factory selected for your part. If a tolerance or a feature cannot be held, we raise it before production, and any change to the drawing is approved by you in writing.

Which documents are supplied?

An inspection report to your drawing or QC sheet, a first-article report when first articles are made, material reports or certificates where required, and photos before and after packing. Material compliance documents on request.

What if my drawing calls up a specific standard?

We place the order with a factory that works to it, and the parts are inspected to it. If a requirement cannot be met, this is stated before the quotation, together with a proposed alternative.

Can anything change without my approval?

No. No change to material, finish, process or drawing interpretation goes ahead without your written approval.

Do we receive photographs before packing?

Yes. Photographs before and after packing are supplied with the inspection report.

Can you supply material certificates?

Material reports or certificates are supplied where your order requires them; state the requirement in the RFQ.
