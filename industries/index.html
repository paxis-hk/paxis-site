Industries we manufacture for

We manufacture parts to drawing for equipment makers and OEMs. Below are the sectors we work for, the parts they order, and the requirements that matter in each. We do not publish customer names; your drawings and your supply chain remain confidential.

01

Optics and electro-optics

Mechanical parts for optical instruments and electro-optical systems, where stable dimensions and dark, low-reflection surfaces matter.

Parts
Lens holders, adapter rings, spring holders, black-finished housings and covers.
What matters
Cosmetic surfaces, black finishes named exactly, dimensions that stay stable after finishing.
Relevant processes
  • CNC machining
  • Custom springs
  • EVA and custom packaging

02

Medical and ophthalmic devices

Parts manufactured to drawing for device makers. We do not design or certify medical devices; we manufacture parts to your drawing and the requirements on it.

Parts
Housings, covers, holders and machined mechanical parts.
What matters
Material reports, clean finishes, documentation agreed before the order.
Relevant processes
  • CNC machining
  • Plastic parts and injection molds
  • Sheet metal and laser cutting

03

Laser and industrial equipment

Machined and fabricated parts for laser systems and industrial machines.

Parts
Machined housings, sheet-metal enclosures, brackets, panels.
What matters
Flatness, hole positions across assemblies, painted and anodized cosmetic faces.
Relevant processes
  • CNC machining
  • Sheet metal and laser cutting
  • Assemblies and kits

04

Power electronics

Enclosures and mechanical parts for power conversion and control equipment.

Parts
Enclosures, panels, heat-sink and mounting parts, insulating parts.
What matters
Conductive and insulating materials named exactly, bare contact faces, press-fit hardware.
Relevant processes
  • Sheet metal and laser cutting
  • CNC machining
  • Plastic parts and injection molds

05

Production and automation equipment

Machine parts and spares for production lines and automated equipment.

Parts
Machine parts, springs, jigs and fixtures, spare parts.
What matters
Hardened wear parts, repeatable fixtures, spares that match the original drawing revision.
Relevant processes
  • CNC machining
  • Jigs and fixtures
  • Custom springs

06

Electronics

Mechanical parts and packaging for electronic products.

Parts
Enclosures, covers, panels, packaging inserts.
What matters
Cosmetic finishes, silk-screen printing, packaging that protects the product.
Relevant processes
  • Sheet metal and laser cutting
  • Plastic parts and injection molds
  • EVA and custom packaging

07

Robotics

Structural and motion parts for robots and handling systems.

Parts
Machined structural parts, brackets, shafts.
What matters
Light structural parts, bearing seats, tolerances across mating parts.
Relevant processes
  • CNC machining
  • Sheet metal and laser cutting
  • Assemblies and kits

08

Energy

Machined and fabricated parts for energy equipment.

Parts
Machined and sheet-metal parts to drawing.
What matters
Material reports, corrosion protection, finishes for outdoor use.
Relevant processes
  • CNC machining
  • Sheet metal and laser cutting
  • Jigs and fixtures

09

Automotive

Parts and production aids for automotive equipment and tooling.

Parts
Fixtures, machined parts, molded parts.
What matters
Repeatability between batches, fixtures for assembly and inspection.
Relevant processes
  • Jigs and fixtures
  • CNC machining
  • Plastic parts and injection molds

Sectors not listed here are welcome. We manufacture to drawing, and the requirements of the part are decisive. Send the drawing and we confirm in writing whether we can produce it.
