About PAXIS

Making China's manufacturing accessible to Western industry.

China's factories have the machines, the capacity and the cost that Western manufacturers need. What is often missing is a way in: someone who reads the drawing, picks the right factory, asks the right questions, and checks the parts before they leave.

That is what PAXIS does. You work with us in English, on your terms and to your drawing. We work with the factories on the floor, in Chinese.

A Hong Kong company under Western ownership

PAXIS is the trade name of HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD, a Hong Kong company under Western ownership. Our team has been making parts in China since 2007, and PAXIS carries that work forward.

We make custom parts to customer drawings through a network of vetted factories in southern and eastern China. We do not own factories. Our value is the team that works inside them.

Company facts

PAXIS (HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD) is a Hong Kong company under Western ownership. It makes custom parts to customer drawings through vetted factories in China, with its own team in Dongguan, Guangdong. Its team has been making parts in China since 2007.

Our team in China

Our team in Dongguan places each job, follows production, inspects, packs and ships. They are at the factories in person, not only on the phone.

  • Purchasing and factory follow-up: placing jobs, tracking dates, weekly RFQ status
  • Drawing questions: translating factory questions and your answers, in writing
  • Quality: on-site checks, inspection reports, photos before and after packing
  • Shipping: packing, export paperwork, handover to your forwarder

Who we work for

Engineering and purchasing teams at Western manufacturers who need parts made to drawing, from prototypes to repeat production. Our customers work in optics and electro-optics, medical and ophthalmic devices, laser and industrial equipment, power electronics, production and automation equipment, electronics, robotics and energy. We don't publish their names.
