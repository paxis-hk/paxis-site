About PAXIS

China's factories have the machines, the capacity and the cost that Western manufacturers need. What Western companies need in addition is a supplier that reviews the drawing, selects the right factory, resolves technical questions before production, and verifies the parts before they are released. That is the role of PAXIS.

01Who we are

A Hong Kong company under Western ownership

PAXIS is the trade name of HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD, a Hong Kong company under Western ownership. We manufacture custom parts to customer drawings in a network of vetted partner factories in southern and eastern China, supervised and inspected by our own team in China. Through our network of vetted partner factories we manufacture across the full range of technologies and materials available in Chinese industry. If it can be manufactured in China, we can manufacture it to your drawing.

You work with us in English, on your terms and to your drawing, and you pay in US dollars. With the factories we work in Chinese: our team includes native Chinese speakers fluent in English, so nothing is lost in translation.

02What we do

What we do, and what we do not do

  • We review your drawing and raise questions before the quotation.
  • We select the qualified factory for your part and issue one quotation.
  • Our team supervises production and verifies quality at the factory.
  • PAXIS is responsible for the parts: one supplier, one contact.
  • We do not own factories.
  • We do not design parts; we manufacture to your drawing.
  • We do not arrange transport; orders are ready at the factory, EXW.

03History

Manufacturing in China since 2007

We have been manufacturing parts to customer drawings in China since 2007. PAXIS, as HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD, carries that work forward under one name: the same way of working, the same attention to the drawing, and factories we visit several times a year.

06For customers

What our customers receive

  1. 01

    Quality

    Every order is controlled in two independent stages: the manufacturer's inspection to your drawing or QC sheet, and our own verification at the factory before the goods are released.

  2. 02

    Precision

    Each part goes to a factory equipped for its process and tolerances, from 5-axis machining to laser cutting and injection molding.

  3. 03

    On schedule

    We commit to the schedule confirmed in your order. Production is followed at the factory against that schedule, and any risk to a date is reported to you without delay.

  4. 04

    Price

    We select the right qualified factory for your part: the best quality at the most competitive price. You receive one quotation from PAXIS.

  5. 05

    Payment in US dollars

    You pay PAXIS in US dollars. No currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China.
