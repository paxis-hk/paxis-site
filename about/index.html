About PAXIS

Making China's manufacturing accessible to Western industry.

China's factories have the machines, the capacity and the cost that Western manufacturers need. What is often missing is a way in: someone who reads the drawing, selects the right factory, asks the right questions, and verifies the parts before they leave the factory.

That is what PAXIS does. You work with us in English, on your terms and to your drawing, and you pay in US dollars. Our team works with the factories in Chinese.

A Hong Kong company under Western ownership

PAXIS is the trade name of HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD, a Hong Kong company under Western ownership. Our team has been manufacturing parts in China since 2007, and PAXIS carries that work forward.

We manufacture custom parts to customer drawings through a network of vetted factories in southern and eastern China. We do not own factories. Our value is the team that supervises production and quality at them.

Company facts

PAXIS (HONG KONG PRIMEAXIS PRECISION LTD) is a Hong Kong company under Western ownership. It manufactures custom parts to customer drawings through vetted factories in China, with its own team in China supervising production and quality. Its team has been manufacturing parts in China since 2007.

What our customers get

  • Quality: parts inspected by the factory against your drawing, then verified by our team at the factory before packing.
  • Precision: each part goes to a factory equipped for its process and tolerances, including 5-axis machining and laser cutting.
  • On schedule: we commit to the schedule agreed in your order and tell you early if a date is at risk.
  • Price: the right qualified factory for your part, with the best quality at the most competitive price, and one quote from PAXIS.
  • Payment in US dollars: no currency conversions and no payments to suppliers in China.

Our team in China

Our team in China supervises production and verifies quality at the factories. They visit the factories in person, not only on the phone.

  • Production follow-up: placing orders with the factories, tracking the agreed schedule, weekly RFQ status
  • Drawing questions: translating factory questions and your answers, in writing
  • Quality control: checks at the factory, inspection reports, photos before and after packing
  • Collection: coordinating EXW collection with your forwarder or courier
